# Cart Website

## Checklist

- **Item name and image**: Each item shown with its name and image, matching what was displayed on the product page.
- **Update or remove item**: The ability to adjust quantity, change variants, or remove an item directly from the cart.
- **Item variations**: Selected variants for each item — size, colour, style, or type — displayed clearly alongside the item.
  A mismatch between what the user selected and what appears in the cart is a common source of disputes — the variant should always be clearly visible.
- **Promo or discount code**: A text field for entering a promo, discount, or coupon code.
  Showing whether the code was accepted or rejected immediately after entry removes any ambiguity.
- **Accepted payment methods**: The payment methods available for completing the purchase — cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, and others.
  Displaying recognised payment method logos increases trust at the most anxious point of the purchase flow.
- **Link to support**: A way to get help with items in the cart — particularly useful for products with configurable options or custom orders.
- **Checkout button**: A prominent button to proceed to payment when the user is ready to complete their order.
- **Suggested add-ons**: Related or complementary items suggested based on what is already in the cart.
