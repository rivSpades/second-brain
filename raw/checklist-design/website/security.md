# Security Website

## Checklist

- **Certifications and compliance**: Logos and names of security certifications held e.g. SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, HIPAA
  Certification logos should be paired with an explanation of what they cover
- **Data encryption**: A plain-language explanation of how data is encrypted at rest and in transit
- **Data residency**: Where customer data is stored and processed, with any regional options available
- **Access controls**: Who within the company can access customer data, under what conditions, and what controls are in place to limit that access
- **Vulnerability disclosure**: How security issues are reported and handled e.g. a dedicated email address, a bug bounty programme, or a responsible disclosure policy
- **Incident history**: A record of past security incidents that were disclosed, with dates and resolution summaries for transparency
- **Penetration testing**: Details of third-party security audits or penetration tests, with the most recent date and ability to request a report for further details
