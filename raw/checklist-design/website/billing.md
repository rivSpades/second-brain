# Billing Website

A screen where users manage information regarding payment, subscription and billing.

## Checklist

- **Payment method/s used**: Display payment method details with ability to update or remove. A subscription service may want to prevent all methods being removed as it will become an issue later.
- **History**: A list of past charges with the ability to download invoices
  Downloadable invoice format should be PDF to be easily shared in other reporting tools
- **Plan details**: Details of current plan includes price, features and next billing date
  Have a clear action to upgrade or change plan in this area
- **Promo or discount code**: A field to apply a code to activate a discount or deal
- **Additional details**: Information that influences billing or plan decision making e.g. seats, roles, usage, metrics
- **Next payment**: If subscription, show details of upcoming payment including amount, date and method
- **Cancel subscription**: A clear action to cancel and stop payments
